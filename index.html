Nashville Community Safety Network is a group of individuals organizing around the surveillance issues that disproportionately impact our most oppressed neighbors.

We are and will remain unpaid organizers dedicated to ending the use of any and all methods of surveillance, including personnel, hardware, software, and platforms that perform surveillance functions by collecting, scraping, and/or selling our data.

Our core group is made up of folks who are abolitionists, meaning we believe in and organize for a world where everyone is free -- in reality, not just in theory.

We are committed to this world without cops, courts, and cages, where everyone has the resources they need to survive and thrive.
